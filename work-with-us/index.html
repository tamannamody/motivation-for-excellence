For NGOs

What we look for.

We partner with organisations that implement programmes on the ground inhealthcare, education & training, livelihood enhancement and nurturing excellence, especially with rural, tribal and low-income communities.

  • Values

    Integrity, and respect for the communities a programme serves, come first.

  • Effectiveness

    Programmes that deliver results on the ground, not only on paper.

  • Validation

    Evidence that an approach works, and the discipline to keep measuring it.

  • Innovation

    New ideas that are pragmatic enough to work, and to grow.

  • Excellence

    The pursuit of quality in every activity, however humble.

Getting in touch

Start with a short note.

There is no application form. Write to us at info@motivationforexcellence.org with a page or two covering the points below, and we will be in touch if there is a fit.

  1. 1

    Who you are

    Your organisation, its registration, and the communities you work with.

  2. 2

    The programme

    What you do, where, and which of our four areas it fits.

  3. 3

    The evidence

    What has worked so far, and how you measure it.

  4. 4

    The ask

    What support would help, over what period, and what it would make possible.
