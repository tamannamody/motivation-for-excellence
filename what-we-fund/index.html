How we decide

Every grant passes the same five tests.

Whatever the area, a partner earns our support the same way.

  1. 01Values

    Integrity, and respect for the communities a programme serves, come first.

  2. 02Effectiveness

    Programmes that deliver results on the ground, not only on paper.

  3. 03Validation

    Evidence that an approach works, and the discipline to keep measuring it.

  4. 04Innovation

    New ideas that are pragmatic enough to work, and to grow.

  5. 05Excellence

    The pursuit of quality in every activity, however humble.

Doing work that deserves to grow?

We partner with organisations building innovative, pragmatic and cost-effective solutions for India’s rural and tribal communities. We also welcome philanthropists, companies and foundations who want to co-fund what works.
